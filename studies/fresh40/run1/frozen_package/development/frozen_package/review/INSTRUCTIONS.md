# Twenty-item review timing exercise

The candidate CSV contains five old items per relation selected by a fixed hash key before new development outcomes. They are inherited candidates, not newly verified facts. Two independent identified humans should each complete one row per item in review_form.csv. Keep timing_key.csv with the coordinator, separately from reviewer copies. Reviewers must not see the new model outcomes, report scores or development analysis.

Check subject identity, a single defensible factual reference P, correct semantic types, and C/I being distinct from P and from each other, incorrect under the stated question convention, and not aliases or additional valid answers. Locate authoritative factual evidence and an independent corroborating source when available; record links, retrieval dates, snapshot hashes, assistance and uncertainty truthfully. Do not automatically approve the inherited Wikidata values. Resolve ambiguous entities, multiple developers/languages/networks, time-dependent interpretation and unsupported claims on factual evidence.

Use APPROVE, REJECT or QUERY. Record real reviewer identifiers and actual elapsed minutes. Record AI assistance; it is not a second independent human reviewer. The coordinator logs disagreements and adjudication minutes separately. A timing pilot estimates staffing and yield; it does not replace the complete old-100 re-review or the fresh-candidate review.

No decision, identity, source verification, agreement rate or annotation time is prefilled. New reviews have not been performed by this package. The reference 187-person-hour budget remains an assumption until timed work is supplied.
