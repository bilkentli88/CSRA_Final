## Defensible working contribution — an inference from this bounded search

A controlled 2 x 2 x 2 decomposition of ownership wording, hedging and answer availability in forced-choice source reports, paired with a prospectively specified test of incremental predictive information about a document-substitution effect, would make the revised question more specific. The existing corrected audit supplies motivation, not independent validation of new pilot effects. All three pilot systems remain development systems at one quantization setting.

Queries included combinations of “source reports”, “ownership”, “hedging”, “source attribution”, “answer visibility”, “SELF” and “PEER”. No exact match for the complete planned design was identified among the returned primary sources. That negative search result does not establish firstness; repeat citation chaining and searches before submission. Novelty, empirical strength and venue fit remain separate judgments.

## Consequences for the manuscript

Replace broad claims about introspection or a novel predictive-faithfulness criterion with the operational source-report question. Discuss the four closest papers in the introduction and related work; distinguish their targets and controls rather than implying they are inferior. Preserve the distinction between behavioral intervention sensitivity, predictive redundancy and privileged self-access. Defer any Study 2 results paragraph until the registered pilot actually runs. A polished narrative alone does not resolve small-system coverage, item provenance, or independent factual-review limitations.
