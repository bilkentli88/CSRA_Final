## Experimental work still open

2. Broader independently checked prospective evaluation: determine sample size from precision or simulation, preserve item/group separation and fixed development procedures, and avoid selecting the sample to force a favorable confidence interval.
3. Broader model evaluation if resources permit, with independently specified target and reporter choices.

These are research extensions, not completed findings or claims that a journal formally requires a particular new experiment. The archived V6 research plan is retained at provenance/prior_releases/V6_KAIS_revision_plan.md.
